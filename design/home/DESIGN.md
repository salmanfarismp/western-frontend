## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a warm, tactile, and upscale lifestyle e-commerce atmosphere centered around slow outdoor living, curated patio sanctuaries, and artisanal craft. Designed for discerning homeowners, interior designers, and outdoor enthusiasts seeking leisure, comfort, and architectural serenity, the visual language balances organic textures with high-end editorial refinement.

Drawing inspiration from tactile organic minimalism and quiet luxury, the aesthetic integrates soothing sun-dappled neutrals, grounding botanical sage tones, rich espresso silhouettes, and sunbaked terracotta accents. Surfaces feature warm linen textures, airy negative space, rhythmic grid alignments, and gentle tactile transitions. The experience feels welcoming, relaxed, enduring, and sophisticated.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in earth tones directly informed by the logo mark and upscale botanical outdoor spaces. 

- **Primary (`#627361`)**: Organic sage green, establishing calm, nature-centric brand recognition across primary CTAs, active highlights, and key brand motifs.
- **Secondary (`#251B17`)**: Deep rich espresso, drawn directly from the hanging swing chair structure. It replaces harsh pure blacks with an organic, grounding tone for typography, framing, and heavy interaction moments.
- **Tertiary (`#C2795B`)**: Sunbaked terracotta, used selectively for badges, curated promotional labels, alert states, and seasonal accents.
- **Neutral (`#76746E`)**: Balanced warm mineral gray for secondary UI copy, structural dividers, and supporting metadata.
- **Canvas & Surface System**: Foundations are built on `canvas-linen` (`#FAF8F5`) and `surface-cream` (`#F3EFEA`), providing soft, ambient warmth instead of sterile digital whites. Pure white is reserved strictly for product visual cards (`surface-card`) to showcase photography with pristine clarity.

## Typography

The typographical pairing expresses architectural elegance and ergonomic readability:

- **Headlines (`Playfair Display`)**: Emulates editorial lookbooks, premium shelter magazines, and traditional furniture craftsmanship. It delivers visual gravitas, high-contrast stroke transitions, and classical proportion to titles and marketing statements.
- **Body & Interface (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Provides a friendly, clear, geometric counterpoint. Its wide aperture and contemporary proportion guarantee effortless legibility across specifications, navigation links, filters, checkout inputs, and pricing displays.
- **Labels & Tags**: Rendered in uppercase or small-caps with deliberate letter-spacing (`0.02em` - `0.06em`) to convey restraint, order, and curated elegance.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is anchored on an editorial 12-column grid for desktop (max canvas width `1440px`), collapsing into an 8-column layout on tablet (`768px - 1024px`) and a 4-column layout on mobile viewports (`< 768px`).

- **Vertical Rhythm**: Generous vertical breathing room between lifestyle sections (`space-xxl` to `6rem`) to maintain an airy, leisurely cadence that avoids dense discount-retail clutter.
- **Product Grids**: Product listing pages default to a 3-column or 4-column balanced grid on desktop, 2-column on tablet, and single or 2-column staggered scroll on mobile.
- **Micro Spacing**: Component internals adhere strictly to 4px/8px increments. Compact elements (chips, pills, buttons) use `space-xs` and `space-sm` for structural tightness, while product cards utilize `space-md` to `space-lg` padding to isolate imagery and pricing cleanly.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses warm, diffuse ambient light rather than artificial cool dropshadows, evoking natural afternoon sun on veranda surfaces:

- **Base Surfaces**: Flat, layered depth created by nesting pure white cards (`#FFFFFF`) against textured linen backgrounds (`#FAF8F5` and `#F3EFEA`).
- **Ambient Shadow (Cards & Interactive Surfaces)**: Extra-soft, widely diffused shadows tinted with espresso rather than neutral gray: `box-shadow: 0 10px 30px -4px rgba(37, 27, 23, 0.05), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(37, 27, 23, 0.03)`.
- **Hover & Floating Depth**: Elevates on interaction with a slow easing curve: `box-shadow: 0 20px 40px -6px rgba(37, 27, 23, 0.09), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(37, 27, 23, 0.04)`.
- **Structural Outlines**: 1px subtle tactile borders (`border-subtle`, `#E4DDD3`) define form boundaries when shadows are absent, preventing harsh separation and maintaining a soft touch.
- **Overlays & Drawers**: Modals, filter sheets, and cart overlays apply a warm tinted backdrop scrim: `rgba(37, 27, 23, 0.45)` with a light `backdrop-filter: blur(4px)`.

## Shapes

The shape language reflects the organic curves seen in egg swing chairs, woven textures, and botanical leaves. 

- **Primary Geometry**: Standard interactive containers, input fields, cards, and modal dialogs adopt a moderate roundedness (`0.5rem` / `8px` base, scaling to `1rem` / `16px` on large cards and hero panels).
- **Organic Accents**: Category circular thumbnails, organic pill badges (`rounded-full`), and smooth product silhouette cutouts contrast with architectural layout lines.
- **Pills**: Product tags (e.g., "Handwoven", "All-Weather Wicker", "New Arrival") utilize fully rounded pill containers to convey artisanal softness.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button**: Solid sage green (`#627361`) background with clean white typography, `0.5rem` border radius, `12px 24px` padding, subtle hover shift to deep moss (`#475546`).
- **Secondary Button**: Outlined border (`1.5px solid #251B17`) with transparent background and rich espresso text. Hover fills with rich espresso and shifts text to linen.
- **Tertiary / Link Button**: Plain espresso text with an animated 1px underline that expands smoothly from left to right on hover.
- **Floating / Icon Button**: Rounded pill or circular surface (`#FFFFFF`), ambient shadow, hosting wishlists or quick-view triggers.

### Chips & Badges
- **Pill Badges**: Set in `label-caption` with fully rounded edges. Available in:
  - *Natural Botanical*: Pale sage background with `#475546` text.
  - *Terracotta Accent*: `#D99377` background at 15% opacity with `#C2795B` text for "Best Seller" or "Limited Edition".
  - *Material Specs*: `#E8DEC8` with `#251B17` text for material highlights like "Solid Teak" or "Sunbrella® Fabric".

### Product Cards
- **Structure**: Clean vertical arrangement with an off-white or white canvas photo tile, subtle 1px border (`#E4DDD3`), gentle corner curvature (`12px`), and minimal metadata.
- **Imagery**: Generous 4:5 or 1:1 lifestyle aspect ratio. Image switches gracefully to secondary angle on hover with a 400ms ease transition.
- **Metadata**: Left-aligned headline in `title-md` (`Playfair Display`), material summary in neutral body-sm, and espresso price display. Quick-add icon button reveals smoothly on card hover.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Text Inputs**: Linen-toned background (`#FAF8F5`) with a 1px border (`#E4DDD3`), `8px` corner radius, rich espresso value text, and sage green focus ring (`2px #627361`).
- **Quantity Pickers**: Horizontal pill shape housing minus, quantity integer, and plus buttons, bounded by a delicate single line border.
- **Swatches**: Circular fabric and finish swatches (32px diameter) with an outer selection ring spaced 2px away when active.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Radio Buttons**: Rich espresso outer circle with a concentric solid dot upon selection.
- **Checkboxes**: Soft-cornered square (4px radius) in sage fill when selected, featuring a crisp white checkmark icon.

### Drawer & Cart Experience
- Slide-out mini-cart with linen canvas background, clear item thumbnails, quantity adjustment controls, and free shipping progress bar filled in primary sage green.
